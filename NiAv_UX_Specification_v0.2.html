5. Common interaction rules

  • Save/hold before destructive navigation where safe.
  • Inline field errors retain entered values.
  • Posted documents do not expose silent delete.
  • Permissions are enforced in the domain layer, not only through hidden buttons.
  • Offline status is visible but does not prevent local core work.
  • Lists are paginated; search is available globally and contextually.
